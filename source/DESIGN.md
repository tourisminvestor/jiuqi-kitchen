# 玖柒廚房 visual system

Impeccable bolder/polish guidance applied to the whole existing product: preserve the green/orange identity, replace timid hierarchy and generic card treatments with a contemporary Hong Kong food-publication aesthetic.

Deep evergreen ink, vivid leaf green and warm mandarin orange, clean off-white reading surface. Self-hosted Noto Serif HK for display and dish titles; local HK sans-serif for controls. No generic English kickers, decorative section numbers, empty icon tiles or decorative gradients.

The first screen exposes products and recipes. Product selector is a compact shelf of real packs. Recipe collections use spacious image-led layouts with a single larger first tile on desktop and a two-column mobile collection. Innovation uses three expressive food stories with ingredients and complete recipes available in the same detail sheet. Navigation is top-aligned on desktop and fixed at the bottom on phones, with four destinations.

Motion is purposeful: navigation underline, gentle panel entrance, saved-heart response, and the card-generation progress state. Reduced-motion users get static transitions. Form controls have visible labels, 44px touch targets, and actionable errors. Main body text 16px; labels 14px; metadata 12px minimum.
